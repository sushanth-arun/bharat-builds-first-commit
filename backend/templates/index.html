Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions about PRAAPTI AI, Cedar Zero-Trust, and statutory RTI drafting.

How does PRAAPTI calculate empirical approval odds? +
Our Strands agent uses statutory Indian welfare eligibility rules, weighting family income ceiling compliance (25%), occupation relevance (30%), age/gender alignment (15%), agricultural landholding (15%), and required document readiness (15%).
Why is Cedar Policy Language used for RTI drafting? +
Cedar enforces fine-grained, zero-trust authorization. It allows public welfare discovery for all citizens, permits verified citizens to draft Tier 1 PIO and Tier 2 FAA notices, but forbids unverified accounts from filing Tier 3 Central Information Commission (CIC) second appeals to prevent automated bot spam.
Is PRAAPTI AI really running 100% locally? +
Yes! Built for Track 1 of the WeMakeDevs AWS Hackathon, the entire stack (OpenSearch, Cedar evaluation, LocalStack, and Strands Agents) runs locally without requiring an AWS cloud account or external cloud egress.
